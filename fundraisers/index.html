The Process

How It Works.

01
Step One
Submit Your Request

Fill out the form below with your organization details, event description, and proposed dates. We review all requests within 2–3 business days.

02
Step Two
Pick a Date & Promote

Once we agree on a date, a flyer gets made (by you or us) and both parties start promoting. Use your social channels, email lists, and network to get people there.

03
Step Three
Raise Money & Have Fun

Guests mention the fundraiser or bring a flyer. We tally everything up and cut you a check for 20% of the night's sales. Simple, transparent, effective.

The Venue Is Yours

Your charity has the venue for the night. Our regular customers become new faces for your cause. You'll have a designated table for promotional materials — let guests come to you.

📣
Marketing & Promotion

We'll put up flyers the day of and can use our social media channels to spread awareness. You bring the outside hustle — we'll back you up inside.

🎨
Banners & Signage

Place banners inside the restaurant and out front. Bring any promotional material that represents your organization — we'll make room for it.

🍺
Full Bar & Kitchen

Your guests enjoy the full TapRoom experience — 50 rotating taps, full food menu, dog-friendly patio. Give your supporters a night they'll talk about.
