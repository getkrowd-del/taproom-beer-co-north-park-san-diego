Beer With

Founded by San Diego natives Kyle & Kevin Conover, TapRoom Beer Co. is a multi-level 5,000 sq ft brewery & restaurant in the heart of North Park. We pour 50 taps of premium craft beer alongside an elevated pub menu — all in a neighborhood environment that feels like home. Dog friendly patio, weekend brunch, and world-class beer brewed right here.
