Pacific Beach

Craft Beer &
Pizza at
the Beach.

TapRoom is a top San Diego Craft Beer Bar & Pizza Restaurant established in 2006 in Pacific Beach. With a focus on San Diego craft beer, awesome food & local hospitality, TapRoom is acclaimed as a local favorite and one of San Diego's top craft beer bars.

We offer a wide range of elevated food from pizza to burgers and tacos, all available for dine-in or delivery. Our large patio is great for large groups and is dog-friendly — just 6 blocks from the beach. With lots of TVs, TapRoom is the place to catch a game or hang out with friends.
